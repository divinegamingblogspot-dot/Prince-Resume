SEARCH + CONTEXT

About Prince Dixit — operations, digital growth and practical technology

Prince Dixit combines business operations with digital execution. His work spans e-commerce management, inventory and order fulfillment, telesales, SEO, digital marketing, website and app troubleshooting, spreadsheet automation and software experiments.

A recurring theme is turning messy operational information into something structured and usable. That can mean organizing product and supplier data, improving an order workflow, building a Google Sheets automation, supporting on-page SEO, connecting a backend with Supabase, or designing a portfolio interface that explains technical work to recruiters and business teams.

Relevant capabilities include e-commerce operations, product data management, inventory management, order processing, digital marketing, SEO, Meta Ads, Google Sheets automation, Apps Script, JavaScript, web development, API integration and AI interface design. The portfolio documents both the business context and the technical layer rather than presenting isolated tools without evidence.

ABOUT · EXPERIENCE + APPROACH

A business-first approach to digital technology

Prince's professional interests sit between operations, marketing, search visibility and technology. Rather than treating each area as separate, he looks for the connection between them: better product data improves e-commerce operations; better information architecture improves SEO; better spreadsheets reduce repetitive work; and better interfaces make systems easier for people to use.

His experience with e-commerce has created practical familiarity with the details behind online business, including product catalogs, SKUs, stock information, suppliers, purchasing, order fulfillment and customer-facing processes. His digital marketing work adds experience with SEO, social media, advertising, content and online visibility.

The technical side includes Google Sheets automation, Google Apps Script, JavaScript, web development, API integration and Supabase-backed applications. These technologies are used as tools to solve specific problems, not simply as a list of programming keywords.

The result is a profile suited to roles involving e-commerce management, digital marketing, SEO, operations, business automation, website management, technical support and digital systems.
