Accessible Interactions for Animated Websites

Practical keyboard, focus, contrast and reduced-motion patterns for portfolios with rich interactive interfaces.

Keyboard UXFocusReduced Motion

1. Keyboard access is a first-class path

Every interactive control should be reachable and operable without a mouse. Use native buttons and links where possible and make the focus indicator easy to see.

2. Respect reduced motion

Read the user's reduced-motion preference and avoid making essential information depend on movement. Motion should clarify state, not block reading or navigation.

3. Design for contrast and clarity

Check text and controls against every theme, including hover, focus, disabled and active states. Never use accent color alone to communicate an important state.

4. Test the real journey

Navigate the whole page with Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space and Escape. Test menus, dialogs, forms, assistant widgets and mobile touch targets.
