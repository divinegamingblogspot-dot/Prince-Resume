Internal Links as a Knowledge Graph

How related pages, projects, skills and articles can help people and search engines understand a portfolio.

Internal LinksKnowledge GraphSEO

1. Link relationships, not just destinations

A project page should point to relevant skills, experience, systems and articles. Each link should help the visitor answer a sensible next question.

2. Build topic clusters

Group articles around clear themes such as automation, SEO, e-commerce and performance. Connect supporting articles to the main topic without forcing unrelated links.

3. Use descriptive link text

Prefer labels that explain the destination over repeated vague phrases. Keep navigation consistent and ensure links work on both desktop and mobile.

4. Keep the graph honest

Only connect pages when the relationship is real. Structured data and internal links should describe visible content, not imply achievements or relationships that are not present.
