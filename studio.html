HOW PROJECTS WORK

Small, clear steps. No mystery process.

01 · Understand

Clarify the goal, audience, current tools, constraints and what success should look like.

02 · Scope

Agree on deliverables, exclusions, access, timeline and the simplest useful first version.

03 · Build

Implement in stages, protect existing workflows and share a working preview where possible.

04 · Verify

Test the important journeys, document limits and hand over what was actually delivered.
