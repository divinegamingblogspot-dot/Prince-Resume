Operational Spreadsheet Systems

Structured product, SKU, inventory, vendor and purchasing workbooks

Business spreadsheets designed as controlled operating systems rather than simple tables.

SystemsAutomationTechnologyBuild

Deep dive

Scope

Product and SKU data, inventory, vendor information, purchasing coordination, formulas, validation and operational status flows.

Design approach

Preserve existing columns and formulas where required, isolate automation to defined fields, make workflows visible and reduce accidental data damage.

Automation layer

Image synchronization, formula-driven fields, validation, controlled updates and Apps Script helpers can sit around the workbook without changing the team's source-of-truth structure.

Outcome

Operational data becomes easier to maintain, audit and extend while repetitive work is shifted into repeatable logic.

How it fits the bigger picture

This build is part of a broader toolkit connecting business operations, data, automation, AI, software and web engineering.
