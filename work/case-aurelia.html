A storefront designed around product discovery

Retail storefront · product discovery · customer journey

AURELIA is a women’s accessories retail storefront built to demonstrate a richer shopping journey than a simple product grid, while keeping the experience responsive and navigable.

Project breakdown

01

Goal

Build a coherent retail experience for women’s accessories that lets visitors browse, narrow, compare and explore products. The project is a retail storefront—not a wholesale pricing portal—and is intended to demonstrate product experience and front-end system design.

02

Discovery and catalogue

The experience includes category navigation, a broad product catalogue, search with suggestions, filtering, product galleries and quick-view interactions. These features aim to reduce the effort between arriving at the store and finding a relevant item.

03

Shopping support

Wishlist, recommendations, a gift finder and a look builder give visitors different paths into the catalogue. Delivery PIN checks and account/customer flows provide the structure of a more complete shopping journey.

04

Responsive and visual system

The interface is designed to work across desktop and mobile navigation, with multiple visual themes. Shared components and consistent states help the storefront feel like one system rather than a collection of disconnected screens.

05

Checkout boundary

The project includes a demo checkout/customer flow. It should not be represented as a live payment-processing or production fulfilment system unless those integrations are separately implemented and verified.

06

What this demonstrates

AURELIA shows how catalogue structure, interaction design and responsive presentation can be combined in a retail experience. It is a portfolio build; no sales, conversion-rate or customer-retention outcome is claimed.
