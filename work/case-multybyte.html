Project breakdown

01

Context

Multybyte operates a wholesale e-commerce workflow where product information and supplier purchasing details need to stay usable across office, warehouse and dispatch work. The existing sheet is an operational source of truth, so the solution must fit around it rather than force a replacement.

02

The problem to solve

Repeated product checks create avoidable handling. Product identifiers, names, quantities, supplier names, product URLs and image references need to remain aligned. A useful automation must also avoid overwriting trusted columns, formulas or vendor-specific information.

03

Design decisions

The working sheet is organised around SKU, product name, quantity, supplier, product link and image. The image-sync workflow reads the product link and writes only the intended image output. The vendor area is treated as protected data rather than a general-purpose automation target.

04

Reliability and safeguards

The design prioritises bounded retries, cache-aware lookups, locking against overlapping runs, explicit target columns and a backup/restore mindset. Failures should be visible and recoverable; a partially successful run must not silently rewrite unrelated fields.

05

What was built

A Google Sheets and Apps Script-based workflow supporting product-data handling and image synchronisation. The broader purchasing system is documented separately from listing-price discount slabs so operational rules are not accidentally mixed.

06

Evidence and limits

This page describes the workflow design and implemented project scope, not a measured business-impact report. No percentage time savings, revenue lift or error-reduction figure is claimed because a verified before/after measurement has not been published.
